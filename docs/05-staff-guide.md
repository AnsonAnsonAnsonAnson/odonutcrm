# Staff guide: running the O! Donut Club at the counter

A one-page version prints from **Admin → Print kit → Staff cheat sheet**. This guide is the longer version for training a new team member (about 20 minutes).

## Set up the counter device (once)

1. Use any tablet or phone with a camera. Open `https://your-domain/staff` and sign in with your own staff account (each person has their own; never share logins).
2. Add it to the home screen (Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen; Chrome: ⋮ → Add to Home screen) so it opens full screen.
3. Allow camera access when asked. Keep it charging. If the camera is not available, type the code under the QR instead.

## The 10-second flow

| Step | Say / do |
|---|---|
| 1 | "Are you an O! Donut Club member?" |
| 2 | **Yes:** they show the card in Apple Wallet, Google Wallet or the browser. Scan the QR. No phone? Search their mobile number. |
| 3 | Check the name on screen, type the **bill total** (for example 85) and tap **Add stamps**. The screen works out the stamps: one for every $40, rounded down, so $85 gives 2. To add stamps without a bill, tap **Number of stamps** and use − / +. |
| 4 | Read out the result: "That's 7 of 10, three more for a free donut." If a card completes, the screen says **Card complete** and the free donut voucher is already in their account. |
| — | **No:** "Scan the poster, it takes 30 seconds and you get a free topping today." Stamp this purchase once they have joined (scan their new card). |

The screen shows the member's tier (Sprinkle, Glazed, Golden). If it says **Now a Glazed member!** after a stamp, congratulate them: they just got a tier reward.

## Rewards and vouchers

1. The customer opens **My Vouchers** in their Member Center and shows the voucher QR.
2. Scan it. The screen shows the voucher, who it belongs to and its expiry.
3. Tap **Redeem**, then hand over the item. A voucher works once; the system refuses used or expired ones.
4. Do not redeem from a screenshot sent by someone else, or from a code read out loud without typing it in.

## Rules

- **One stamp for every $40 on one bill**, rounded down. At most 5 per scan; a bigger bill needs a manager. (If the owner turns the $40 rule off in Settings, you count stamps with − / + instead.)
- **New members** already have their first stamp and a **Free Cream Cheese Donut** voucher when they reach the counter.
- **A full card (10 stamps)** gives one **2 Free Donuts** voucher. Hand over both donuts together; the voucher works once.
- **Never stamp without a purchase.** Every stamp records your name, the shop and the time.
- During a **bonus stamps** promotion the counter screen shows a yellow strip. Type the real bill total; the bonus is added automatically.
- **Mistakes:** tell a manager. Managers fix them in **Admin → Members → (member) → Adjust stamps** with a reason.
- If the same card was just stamped, the screen asks you to wait a moment. This stops accidental double taps.

## Questions customers ask

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Do I need to download an app? | No. The card goes in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, or you can use it in the browser. |
| I changed phones / lost my card. | Go to the join page and sign in with the same email. Your stamps and vouchers come back. Then add the card to the wallet again. |
| Can I sign in with my phone number? | Yes, if the shop has SMS sign-in switched on; otherwise use your email. |
| Where are my rewards? | Member Center → My Vouchers. The wallet card also shows how many you have. |
| My stamps didn't show up. | Pull down to refresh the Member Center. Wallet cards update within a minute; if it still looks wrong, a manager can check the history. |
| I don't want the messages. | Member Center → tap your initial → switch off offers. You can also delete your account there. |
| Can my friend use my card? | Stamps and vouchers belong to the member. Friends can join with your referral link and you both get a reward after their first purchase. |

## Managers

- **Members:** search, view history, add notes and tags, adjust stamps, gift a voucher, send a message.
- **Dashboard:** check weekly. Watch the wallet add rate (aim for 50%+) and the redemption rate.
- **End of day:** nothing to do. Backups and reminders run by themselves.
